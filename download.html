Requirements

  • macOS 14 Sonoma or newer
  • Apple Silicon or Intel (Universal)
  • ~200 MB free disk
  • 16 GB RAM recommended for 7B local models

First-run setup

  1. Install LM Studio and load a model.
  2. Open Aguacatech. Settings → General → pick model.
  3. Send your first message.
  4. Paste a license key under Settings → License when you upgrade.
On first launch you might see a Gatekeeper warning.

Right-click the app → Open → confirm. macOS remembers the decision for future launches. We're signed with Apple Developer ID and notarized, but Gatekeeper still nags the first time on apps not distributed through the App Store.
